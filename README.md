# Big Ears Trainer

*Big ears* is jazz slang for a player who hears everything. This is ear training for guitar built on the "tonal map": every sound is a number in the key (major home 1, minor home 6).

Hear a note and name its number, sing phrases before you play them, find numbers on the neck, learn chords as numbers, hear progressions, jam over loops, write melodies, and keep a log of songs you've mapped.

**Microphone:** press *Mic* and answer any question by playing or singing the note. Chord questions take the chord's root. The mic pauses while the app is playing; headphones work best. Options (sensitivity, octave matching, singing check in cents, two muted strums = Next) are under *Settings*.

Install it: open the site on your phone and choose *Add to Home Screen*. It works offline once loaded. Songs and melodies are saved on the device; use *Export backup* in the Song log to move them.

Night (midnight and brass) is the default; switch to Day with the sun button in the header.
